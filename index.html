The science

Built on 15 years of VBT research.

POWR doesn't invent the rules. It applies published, peer-reviewed methods with auditable logic — not an AI that guesses.

Velocity predicts %1RM

Mean bar speed tracks very closely what percentage of your max you're lifting.

González-Badillo & Sánchez-Medina, Int J Sports Med, 2010

Less fatigue, same gains

Stopping sets at 20% velocity loss gave similar strength gains to going to 40%, with less fatigue.

Pareja-Blanco et al., Scand J Med Sci Sports, 2017

Phone sensors work

Modern phone accelerometers and gyroscopes sample at 100–200 Hz, enough to resolve the speed of a rep.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is velocity-based training (VBT)?

Training that uses bar speed as the measure of effort. With the same weight, the bar moves faster on a good day than on a bad one. VBT measures that and adjusts your training to how you are today — not to what the plan said.

Which phones does it work with?

Any MagSafe iPhone (iPhone 12 or later) snaps on directly; other phones use the included magnetic ring, which sticks to your case. The app is currently in beta on iPhone and every kit includes access. Android is on the way.

Is it as accurate as a €500 sensor?

For what matters in training — seeing when you slow down and how you progress — it's useful and consistent. A lab linear encoder is still more accurate in absolute terms. That's why we're building the POWR Collar: for those who want that extra precision.

Will my phone fall off?

The clip grips the bar with rubber strips and the magnetic ring is double-sided N52. We test it for load, pull-off and heat. We still don't recommend it for Olympic lifts where the bar is dropped from overhead.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Tracking and history are free. POWR Premium adds deeper analysis, and every kit includes free months of Premium.

How fast is shipping? Can I return it?

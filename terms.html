2. What Wizelift is

Wizelift is an app for velocity-based training (VBT: choosing your load from how fast you move the bar). With it you can:

  • Measure your sets with your phone's motion sensors, attached to the bar (for example with the Wizelift Kit) or to a machine. The app counts reps and calculates velocity, power, range of motion and other metrics.
  • Log and plan workouts, routines and rep-max (RM) tests, and see your history and progress.
  • Use estimates: estimated 1RM, velocity zones, load suggestions and a readiness score calculated on your phone.
  • Connect Apple Health or Health Connect, if you allow it, to read some health readings and save your workouts there.
  • Use the social features: follow people, view and comment on workouts, like them, add photos, share a workout by link, train with a partner, and compare yourself on leaderboards and "segments" if your settings allow it.
  • Export your data (JSON and CSV) and delete your account from the app.

Some features may not be available on every device, language or country, or may be in testing. We will tell you inside the app when a feature is in testing.

5. Health and safety — please read

Wizelift is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or injury. It does not give medical, physiotherapy or nutrition advice. What you see in the app is training information, not health advice.

Lifting weights carries a risk of injury, sometimes serious. So:

  • If you have a medical condition or an injury, are pregnant, or are coming back after a break, talk to a health professional before you start.
  • Warm up, use proper technique and pick loads you can control.
  • For RM tests, sets to failure and maximal lifts, train with safety arms or a spotter.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or chest pain, and get medical help if needed.
  • Fix your phone and its mount securely. Check they cannot come loose during the set. If you use the Wizelift Kit, follow its instructions and warnings.
  • Do not look at your phone while you are lifting a load.

The numbers are estimates. Velocity, reps, estimated 1RM, zones, load suggestions and the readiness score come from your phone's sensors and from calculation models. They can be wrong: because of where the phone sits, the mount, the exercise or the sensor itself. Use them as a guide, not as absolute truth, and choose your load based on how you feel.

Health readings. If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Wizelift uses those readings (for example resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep or weight) only to adapt your training. They are not used to monitor your health or detect medical problems.

5. Salud y seguridad — léelo

Wizelift no es un dispositivo médico. No diagnostica, no trata, no cura ni previene ninguna enfermedad o lesión. No da consejo médico, de fisioterapia ni de nutrición. Lo que ves en la app es información para entrenar, no una recomendación de salud.

Entrenar con cargas tiene riesgo de lesión, a veces grave. Por eso:

  • Si tienes una enfermedad, una lesión, estás embarazada o vuelves a entrenar tras un parón, consulta a un profesional sanitario antes de empezar.
  • Calienta, usa una técnica correcta y elige cargas que puedas controlar.
  • En los tests de RM, series al fallo y levantamientos máximos, entrena con barras de seguridad o con alguien que te asista.
  • Para si sientes dolor, mareo, falta de aire o dolor en el pecho, y busca ayuda médica si hace falta.
  • Sujeta bien el móvil y el soporte. Comprueba que no se pueden soltar durante la serie. Si usas el Wizelift Kit, sigue sus instrucciones y advertencias.
  • No mires el móvil mientras levantas una carga.

Las cifras son estimaciones. La velocidad, las repeticiones, el 1RM estimado, las zonas, las sugerencias de carga y la puntuación de disposición salen de los sensores de tu móvil y de modelos de cálculo. Pueden fallar: por la posición del móvil, el soporte, el tipo de ejercicio o el propio sensor. Úsalas como guía, no como verdad absoluta, y decide tú la carga según cómo te encuentres.

Lecturas de salud. Si conectas Apple Salud o Health Connect, Wizelift usa esas lecturas (por ejemplo, frecuencia cardiaca en reposo, variabilidad de la frecuencia cardiaca, sueño o peso) solo para adaptar tu entrenamiento. No sirven para vigilar tu salud ni para detectar problemas médicos.
